I've had portfolios built for me more times than I can count, and honestly,
I don't think anyone needs one anymore. In the AI era, nobody is really
hunting for raw coding skills. The machines are getting pretty good at that
part. What's left, the part that actually matters, is problem solving,
systems thinking, and storytelling.

So this isn't a portfolio. It's just a place where I document my thoughts and
share the experiences I pick up along the way. No hero images, no "key
achievements," no button that leads to a repo nobody reads. Just short, dated,
honest notes.

The way I see it, if you can convince one person that you can think clearly
through a messy problem, that's worth more than a wall of framework logos ever
was. That's the whole game now.

## What you'll find here

- Notes on problems I've untangled, including the ones I got wrong first
- Thoughts on thinking, leading, and building things that last
- Stories, because the interesting part was never the finished diagram

Follow along if you'd like. If you ever want to reach out, there are some links
over in the [about](#/about) section. I won't bore you past that. Have fun.
